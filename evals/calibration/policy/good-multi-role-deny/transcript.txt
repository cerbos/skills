Cross-role trap: in Cerbos, deny overrides allow only for the SAME role, and an
allow on any other role wins across roles. A person can be BOTH contractor and
employee, and the employee rule allows download. If I scope the deny to
roles:["contractor"] it is defeated by the employee allow and the download is
granted. So I enforce the restriction with a deny scoped to roles:["*"] gated on
a "contractor" in request.principal.roles condition — a wildcard deny applies to
every role the principal holds (including employee), so no cross-role allow can
defeat it. I add a test with a principal holding ["contractor", "employee"] that
asserts EFFECT_DENY.
