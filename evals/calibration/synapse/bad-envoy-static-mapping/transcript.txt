Here's an Envoy ext_authz extension in Starlark. I used the cerbos_mapping mode:
envoy_check returns a Cerbos check_request together with a fixed allow_response
and deny_response, and Synapse sends whichever one matches the decision. On deny
it returns a 403 with a "Forbidden" body.
