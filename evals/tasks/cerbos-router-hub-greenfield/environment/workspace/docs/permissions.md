# Who can do what (v1, signed off by product)

Shifts belong to an organization and a location.

| Action | staff | manager | org_admin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| View a shift | own shifts, plus any published shift at their locations | any shift at locations they manage | any shift in the org |
| Create / edit a shift | no | draft shifts at locations they manage | any shift in the org |
| Publish a schedule | no | locations they manage | any location |
| Request a swap | own published shifts | no | no |
| Approve a swap | no | at locations they manage | any |

Nobody ever sees or touches another organization's data.

Expect changes: two pilot customers have already asked for "senior staff who
can approve swaps" and for managers who can only edit shifts more than 48 hours
out.
